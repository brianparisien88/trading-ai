Wallet activity from the Zerion API, refreshed on the daily sync. A round-trip trade pairs a buy of a token (from a stablecoin, ETH/BNB, or another token) against later sells, FIFO. A token→token swap closes one position and opens another at the swap's USD value. Partial sells leave the remainder as an open holding. LP, staking, bridging and airdrops are ignored; swaps that don't fit a round-trip are counted as unmatched activity. Realized P&L = sum of the matched round-trips below; all-in is Zerion's figure which additionally includes gas, ETH/BNB moves, and unattributed flow. Value change is the raw portfolio delta over the window and includes any deposits/withdrawals. Holdings show our FIFO cost basis; positions acquired by transfer / bridge / wrap show no cost. Positions worth under $3 are hidden. Total Fees = on-chain gas + swap spread (value given up minus value received on each swap — LP fee + slippage + price impact). Each swap's spread is capped at ±3–12% of its size to drop bad thin-token price prints; the ~%/swap shown is the rate measured only on well-priced swaps. Raw un-capped spread runs a bit higher. Prices are USD at block time per Zerion; spam tokens filtered by Zerion. Click a Coin ID to copy the contract address.
