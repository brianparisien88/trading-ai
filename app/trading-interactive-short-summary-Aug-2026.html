Trading Review / 255 trades / Apr-Aug 2026

You're right about the trade. You're wrong about when to sell.

The stock went your way within three weeks on roughly 6 of every 10 trades. You closed in profit on 3.5 of 10. That gap isn't stock-picking. It's exit timing, and it's fixable without changing a single entry.

The evidence / 1 of 3

Being right didn't pay

"Right" = the stock moved at least 10% your way within 20 trading days. "Paid" = the trade closed green.

The evidence / 2 of 3

The winning move is slow

When a trade works, the best exit point arrives on average 14 trading days (about three calendar weeks) after entry. You were often out in two.

The evidence / 3 of 3

Just holding longer flips the book

Same entries, same trades. Replace the two-day exit with a fixed hold and re-price the options. Nothing else changes.

Why it works even though you'd hold the losers longer too: a losing option can't drop past -100%, you only paid the premium. A winning option can run +100% to +300%. Hold everything longer and the extra downside is capped; the extra upside isn't.

What turned out not to matter

  • The setup "score." The top-rated setups won less often than the middle-rated ones. It doesn't predict outcomes, don't screen on it.
  • Contract length, on its own. Long-dated contracts were just a tell that you'd already chased a hot, extended move. The problem was the chase, not the expiry.

The fix

Six rules

Every one is about what you do after you're in. The entries can stay exactly as they are.

  1. Minimum hold: three weeks.~15 trading days on anything that isn't stopped out. Closer to four weeks for beaten-down "bottom-fish" entries, those develop slowest.
  2. Use a time-stop, not a flinch.If it hasn't moved your way by day 10, then reassess. Don't bail on day 2 just because the position is red, that's when the move usually hasn't started.
  3. Don't chase.Skip entries already up more than ~25% in the last month, or more than ~40% off their 3-month low. If you take one anyway: small size and a hard stop, the losses there are lopsided.
  4. Lean into your real edge.Beaten-down reversal / bottom-fish setups are your strongest book (~65%+ and holding). Take more of those, fewer momentum chases.
  5. Buy the right contract.At-the-money or slightly in-the-money, roughly two months out. Not cheap far-out-of-the-money lottery tickets, they need a bigger move just to break even.
  6. Stop scoring the setup.It isn't a useful filter. Spend that attention on the hold instead.

Basis: 255 closed options round-trips, April-August 2026. Realized -$824 after commissions, 36% win rate, 0.96 profit factor.
Method: "worked" = a 10%-plus favourable underlying move within 20 trading days. The hold counterfactual re-prices each contract with Black-Scholes (implied vol from the actual entry premium, -15% haircut on calls for the typical IV drop on a rally). Directional, not a guaranteed number.
